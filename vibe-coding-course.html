The 15-week journey

From first build to career-ready portfolio.

Four units carry you from mindset to a public, employer-facing showcase.

Weeks 1–3

Foundations & Mindset

What vibe coding is, systems thinking, and how AI reshapes your creativity & learning.

Weeks 4–8

Core Craft

The build loop plus websites, PWAs, extensions, data apps & disposable software. Midterm portfolio due.

Weeks 9–12

Prototype → Product

Where PoCs fit in UX & engineering, building AI features on LLMs, and local vs. vended models on IU's REALLMS.

Weeks 13–15

Career & Capstone

Map skills to a real job, prep interviews, and launch your final portfolio site on demo day.
