AI Service or Integration Request

* Indicates required

Use this form to ask UITS for an AI tool or change that isn't available to you today, including:

  • Buying or licensing a new AI tool
  • Turning on a feature that's disabled in an IU AI tool, such as ChatGPT Edu, Google Gemini, or Microsoft Copilot
  • Changing how a UITS-supported AI tool is configured or managed
  • Connecting an AI tool to another IU system

Tell us the problem you're solving. We'll first check whether a tool IU already supports can do it.

The request form questions appear here. See Request form preview for the full form.

Please note

UITS AI Innovation will respond within 2 business days. Requests for new tools or features are reviewed by IU's AI governance group and may need security, privacy, or data steward review.

New purchases also require the Software and Services Selection Process. See About the Software and Services Selection Process (SSSP).

Not sure what you need yet? Try an AI Tool or Productivity Consultation first.
