AI Tool or Productivity Consultation

* Indicates required

Want to get more out of AI tools like Microsoft Copilot, Google Gemini, or ChatGPT Edu? Meet with UITS AI Innovation, on your own or with your team, for practical help using IU's AI tools in your daily work.

In a consultation, we can help you:

  • Pick the right IU-approved tool for a task
  • Write better prompts for your kind of work
  • Find ways to save time on routine tasks
  • Understand which data you can use with each tool

Sessions can be in person or virtual.
