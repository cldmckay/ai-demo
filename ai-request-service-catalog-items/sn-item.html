AI Engineering Consultation

* Indicates required

Planning to build an AI-powered tool, agent, chatbot, or automation? Meet with UITS AI Innovation to talk through your idea, data, and technical options before you start.

In a 30-minute or 1-hour consultation, we can help you:

  • Choose an approach and the right enterprise AI tools
  • Understand data classification and security requirements
  • Pick where your solution should run
  • Identify integrations with IU systems

Before you submit, don't attach files that contain Restricted or Critical data. Describe the data instead.

The request form questions appear here. See Request form preview for the full form.

Please note

After you submit this form, UITS AI Innovation will review it and respond within 2 business days. We aim to meet with you within 5 business days of your request.

A consultation is advice and planning. It is not a commitment by UITS to build, host, or fund your solution.
