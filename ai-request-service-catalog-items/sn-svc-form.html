AI Service or Integration Request

* Indicates required

Use this form to ask UITS for an AI tool or change that isn't available to you today, including:

  • Buying or licensing a new AI tool
  • Turning on a feature that's disabled in an IU AI tool, such as ChatGPT Edu, Google Gemini, or Microsoft Copilot
  • Changing how a UITS-supported AI tool is configured or managed
  • Connecting an AI tool to another IU system
